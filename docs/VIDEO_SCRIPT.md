# Nouri: 3-minute demo video script

**Three things to land:** **emotion** (keep Mum's food and Nani's rituals), **less fuss** (set up once; one scan, one list) and **time saved** (one meal for everyone, buy once). Total run time: about 2:55. Record in English.

## Before you record

- [ ] Open the live site and hard-refresh once (Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + R).
- [ ] Phone, or a desktop browser at phone width (390 px). Light mode, sound on, Do Not Disturb on.
- [ ] Clear the household (Manage → Remove everyone) so the example household with photos shows.
- [ ] Props: a biscuit or namkeen packet whose label lists **milk or nuts** and has a nutrition table.
- [ ] Rehearse the whole flow once right before recording, so the AI answers are warm and fast.
- [ ] If you can, record the screen and your voice separately. Read slowly.

## If something goes wrong

- **The week plan takes about 45 seconds.** Start it, cut the wait in editing, and say over the result: "Ultra plans the whole week, the deepest reasoning in the app."
- **The scan photo is blurry.** Open "What we read", fix a word, and tap Check again. It shows the safety net, so keep it in.
- **The AI is slow or errors.** Tap again (the app retries once on its own), or use "or type the ingredients" and paste:
  `Ingredients: wheat flour (maida), sugar, palm oil, milk solids, hazelnut paste, salt. Nutrition per 100 g: sugars 34 g, sodium 320 mg`
- **Over 3 minutes.** Drop the Invite scene first, then How it works.
- **Never fake a result.** Cut the waits instead.
